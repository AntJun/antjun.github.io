## About This Site

`AntJun's Blog / Akira_Ant's Blog` (referred to below as “this site”) is built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/){:target="\_blank"} and the [TeXt](https://tianqi.name/jekyll-TeXt-theme/){:target="\_blank"} theme.

I created a personal blog in 2017 and later abandoned it for various reasons.

## Names

I usually use Ant Jun as my online name. Akira is an English name I chose for myself—really just a romanization—and I also use Akira Kotori and Akira Ant as nicknames.

### Where the Names Came From

Ant was the name of the first online gaming group I joined, and I have continued using it ever since.
I no longer remember exactly where Akira came from; in Japanese it can mean “dawn.”
Kotori comes from the romanized names of Minami Kotori and Itsuka Kotori. I simply liked Minami Kotori and adopted the name.

## Personality

It is difficult to evaluate my own personality, so take this section as an informal description rather than an objective account.

I am fairly introverted and usually quiet in person. I am not aggressive online either; I mostly add a few comments where I can find common ground. You may notice that many of my accounts contain little beyond profile information. One reason is simple self-interest: I think privately, bookmark things silently, and often do not leave comments or likes. Another is a streak of perfectionism. I worry that a poorly prepared message will create trouble, so I frequently use words such as “probably” and “perhaps.” I know that kind of language is not ideal in technical documentation, which pushes me to verify facts repeatedly. To avoid the hassle, I sometimes stop creating altogether—or create something and never publish it. (~~Even this explanation may not be entirely reliable.~~)

I tend toward pessimistic realism. I am not good at social interaction or verbal expression, and I ~~do not shout and scream~~. I remain neutral on socially sensitive issues.
