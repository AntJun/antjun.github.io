## Donation Policy

- Unless explicitly agreed otherwise, every contribution is treated as an unconditional donation.
- Donations are generally non-refundable.
- Do not use a donation as a reason to request additional personal information from me.
- If your contribution has another purpose, contact me first; otherwise it will be treated as an unconditional donation.
- Check all payment details carefully before paying to avoid fraud.
